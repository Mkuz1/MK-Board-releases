MK Board Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · App: MK Board (com.mkboard.keyboard)

MK Board is a keyboard app for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you type and how the app handles data. In short, MK Board does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any data.

What you type

Everything you type with MK Board stays on your device. MK Board has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything you type, or any other information, off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, MK Board keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Learned words and word history, used to improve suggestions and next-word predictions. MK Board never learns from password fields or from fields that apps mark as private or incognito.
  • Clipboard history, if you use it: the text you copy, so you can paste it again. You can delete clips or turn this feature off.
  • Text shortcuts you create.
  • Settings: languages, colours, layout and an optional background photo you choose.

You can erase learned words in each keyboard's settings ("Clear learned words"). Uninstalling MK Board, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of this data.

Photos

If you choose a background photo, you pick it with Android's own file chooser, and MK Board keeps a copy on your device to draw the keyboard background. MK Board has no access to your other photos or files.

Data collection and sharing

MK Board does not collect personal or sensitive information. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software and no third-party SDKs, and it does not share data with anyone.

Android's keyboard warning

When you turn on any third-party keyboard, Android warns that the keyboard may be able to collect everything you type. That warning appears for every keyboard. MK Board does not and cannot collect what you type, because it has no network access.

Version downloaded from GitHub

This policy describes the Google Play version, which has no internet access. If you install MK Board from GitHub instead, that version also connects to GitHub a few times a day to check for app updates. That check sends nothing you type and no personal information, and you can turn it off on the app's main screen.

Children

MK Board does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
