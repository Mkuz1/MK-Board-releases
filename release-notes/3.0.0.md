## MK Board 3.0.0: smarter typing and a lot more

### Much smarter prediction (Arabic and English especially)
- **Real dictionaries** in all 9 languages (50,000 Arabic words, 45,000 English), stored on the phone and working offline.
- **Arabic-aware:** matching ignores hamza, ة/ه, ى/ي and diacritics. Words with attached و / ف / ب / ل / ك / وال / بال / لل are predicted correctly (والسل → والسلام, للمد → للمدرسة).
- **Next-word prediction:** السلام → عليكم, صباح → الخير, إن → شاء, thank → you. It learns your own phrasing too.
- **Auto-correction:** typos are fixed when you press space (teh → the, السلان → السلام), and Arabic hamza is added (انا → أنا, الى → إلى). Press backspace straight after, or tap your original word, to undo; MK Board then remembers your spelling.
- **Gulf / Emirati vocabulary:** شلون، الحين، وايد، ابغى، زين…
- **Arabizi → Arabic:** on the English keyboard, 7abibi → حبيبي, shukran → شكراً, inshallah → إن شاء الله.
- **Mixed languages:** suggestions from all your enabled languages that share an alphabet, with no 🌐 needed.

### Everyday typing
- **Clipboard history** in the shortcuts panel (Clipboard tab). Items are kept for a day; hold one to pin or delete it. Just-copied text appears as a 📋 paste chip.
- **Emoji:** the full emoji set in iOS-style categories, recently used first, **search** in English or Arabic (🔍 key), and **skin tones** (hold an emoji).
- **Space-bar trackpad:** hold space, then move the cursor in any direction.
- **Incognito typing:** nothing is learned in password or private fields.

### Comfort and looks
- **One-handed mode** on phones (hold 😊, or use Settings).
- **Background photo** behind the keys (Settings → Background photo).
- **Tablets and foldables:** docked, **split** or **floating** keyboard. Hold the keyboard-hide key, or use Settings. Drag the floating keyboard by its handle.
