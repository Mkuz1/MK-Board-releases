## MK Board 2.3.0

- **Navigation buttons stay visible.** The keyboard now sits above Android's back / home / recents bar instead of covering it. The bar takes the keyboard's colour, with buttons in a contrasting shade.
- **Text shortcuts.** Type a shortcut and press space to expand it, e.g. `omw` → "On my way!" or `slm` → "السلام عليكم ورحمة الله وبركاته". The phrase also appears in the suggestion bar while you type.
  - Manage them in each keyboard's settings under **Text shortcuts**: add, edit or delete, or add **ready-made shortcuts** (English and Arabic) in one tap.
  - **Shortcuts button:** tap the speech-bubble key under the iPhone-style keyboard to insert any saved phrase in one tap. On the BlackBerry-style keyboard, hold **sym** or use the button on the sym page.
  - Shortcuts are shared by both keyboards and never leave your phone.
